# Chanasta — Brand & Copy Guide

**Status:** Phase 1 · copy source of truth for UI/UX and engineering
**Owner:** product-architect · **Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Companion docs:** [blueprint.md](./blueprint.md) · [data-model.md](./data-model.md) · [compliance.md](./compliance.md)

Bangla is primary. Every Bangla string below has an English equivalent, and the two are written as **siblings, not translations**: same meaning, natural in each language. Never machine-transliterate Bangla ("সাপোর্ট করুন", "ডোনেট করুন"); if a sentence would not be said by a person in Dhaka, it does not ship.

---

## 1. Brand story

In Bangladesh, you don't usually say *"let me pay you back."* You say:

> **"এক কাপ চা খেয়ে যান।"** — *Have a cup of tea.*

It is the smallest, warmest currency we have. A teacher who stayed late, a friend who listened, a stranger whose tutorial saved your exam — the thank-you is a cup of cha, and if you can stretch it, a plate of nasta to go with it. Tea is never about the tea. It is about saying *"আপনার কাজটা আমার কাজে লেগেছে।"*

**Chanasta is that gesture, made shareable.** The name is **চা (cha) + নাস্তা (nasta)** — tea and snacks. Not a bank, not a charity, not a checkout. The friend who says *"এক কাপ চা খেয়ে যান"* and means it, now with a link the creator can put anywhere.

**Brand promise:** *Support feels like buying a friend a cup of cha — and the money goes straight to them.*
**Brand line (primary):** **এক কাপ চা, অনেক ভালোবাসা। / One cup of cha. A whole lot of love.**

The brand is honest before it is clever: the page's warmth is real, and so is the disclaimer that we never touch the money. Those two things are the brand.

## 2. Name: pronunciation & meaning

| | |
|---|---|
| Bangla | **চানাস্তা** |
| Latin | **Chanasta** (always capital C; lowercase in URLs: `chanasta`, `/@username`) |
| Bangla pronunciation | **চা-নাস-তা** — stress on "নাস": *cha-NAAS-taa* |
| English pronunciation | *chuh-NAH-stuh* ("cha" as in **cha**, "nasta" rhymes with *pasta*) |
| Literal meaning | tea + snacks (চা + নাস্তা) |
| Feeling to protect | warm, everyday, familiar, a little playful — a tea-stall word, not a fintech word |

**Name rules**
- One word, no hyphen, no space. Never "Cha Nasta", "Chaanasta" or "চা-নাস্তা" as the brand lockup (use **চানাস্তা** or **Chanasta**).
- Wordmark: `চানাস্তা` in Bangla contexts, `Chanasta` in English/Latin contexts. Do not put the English wordmark inside Bangla sentences unnecessarily.
- Avoid pairing the name with religious, charity, bank or payment-network iconography. No bKash/Nagad/Rocket logos in the brand lockup.
- Before campaign spend, verify trademark availability and domain/handle availability (Lead decision — [blueprint.md §14](./blueprint.md)).

## 4. Tone of voice

**Personality:** the friend who brings cha to the adda — warm, direct, a little funny, never salesy. Speaks Bangla first. Treats the creator as a person and the supporter as a guest, not a user.

Five rules:

1. **Warm, not gushy.** Short sentences. One idea each. Warmth comes from specificity ("এই ভিডিওটা যদি কাজে লাগে…"), not from exclamation marks.
2. **Honest, not alarming.** Say what we don't do once, plainly, and move on. Trust copy is calm and factual — never a scary legal wall above the fold.
3. **Respectful by default.** Use **আপনি** for supporters in all product copy. No sarcasm, no guilt, no "আপনি না দিলে…".
4. **Concrete, not corporate.** "টাকা সোজা তাঁর অ্যাকাউন্টে যায়" beats "নিরাপদ পেমেন্ট সলিউশন". No jargon, no English-heavy manager-speak.
5. **Low-hype.** No "বিপ্লব", "গেম চেঞ্জার", "সেরা", "নিশ্চিত", "গ্যারান্টি". Understatement reads as confidence here.

**Do / don't examples**

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "নিরাপদ ও ঝুঁকিমুক্ত পেমেন্ট করুন!" | "টাকা সরাসরি ক্রিয়েটরের অ্যাকাউন্টে যাবে — চানাস্তা কোনো টাকা নেয় না।" |
| "আপনার প্রিয় ক্রিয়েটরকে ডোনেট করুন" | "আপনার প্রিয় ক্রিয়েটরকে এক কাপ চা খাওয়ান" |
| "পেমেন্ট সফল হয়েছে!" | "আপনার বার্তাটি পাঠানো হয়েছে। টাকার বিষয়টি চানাস্তা যাচাই করে না।" |
| "১০০% ভেরিফাইড ক্রিয়েটর" | "QR-এর তথ্য যাচাই করা হয়েছে। টাকা এসেছে এমন নয়।" |
| "Guaranteed support, instant settlement" | "Support, directly. Money goes to their account." |
| "আপনি না দিলে ওর কাজ বন্ধ হয়ে যাবে" | "ভালো লাগলে এক কাপ চা খাওয়াতে পারেন।" |

**Punctuation & formatting**
- Bangla sentence end: `।`; English: `.`
- No all-caps, no repeated `!!!`, max one `!` per screen (none in trust/legal copy).
- Numbers: **Latin digits** in UI and microcopy (`৳20`, `৳50`, `৳100,000`) because supporters compare amounts against bKash/Nagad apps that use Latin digits. Render amounts through the engine's `formatTaka()` (e.g. `৳1,250.50`, `৳100,000`) so grouping is consistent; Bangla digits and lakh grouping (`১,০০,০০০ টাকা`) are allowed only in long-form prose/marketing copy. Currency: `৳` before the amount, no space (`৳20`); when spelled out: `২০ টাকা`.
- Provider names: `bKash` (not Bkash/BKASH), `Nagad`, `Rocket`, `Bangla QR` in Latin contexts; `বিকাশ`, `নগদ`, `রকেট`, `বাংলা QR` in Bangla copy.
- No emoji in trust, legal or error copy. Emoji allowed in success states and dashboard onboarding (one max).
- English toggle label: show the *other* language — in Bangla UI the toggle reads **English**, in English UI it reads **বাংলা**.

## 5. Vocabulary (decisions)

We pick words deliberately because they carry legal and emotional weight.

| Concept | **Use** | **Never use** | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| The supporter's act | **সমর্থন / support** | দান / donation; অনুদান | Donation = charity (separate regulated area; [compliance.md §7](./compliance.md)); "support" is the honest frame |
| The fun, primary action | **এক কাপ চা খাওয়ান / buy a cha** | টিপ দিন / tip | "Tip" is a restaurant/card concept and implies a platform payment; cha is the local gesture |
| Money transfer | **পাঠান / send** (in the supporter's own app) | চেকআউট, পেমেন্ট করুন (as our CTA) | "Payment" belongs to the provider's app, never to Chanasta's button |
| The claim | **স্ব-ঘোষিত / self-reported**; "আমি পাঠিয়েছি / I sent it" | "পেমেন্ট সফল", "যাচাই করা পেমেন্ট", "নিশ্চিত পেমেন্ট" | We cannot verify money; claim status must never read as proof |
| Verification | **যাচাই = data check** (QR payload, checksum, merchant fields) | "verified payment", "নিশ্চিত পেমেন্ট" | Reserve the word for what we actually inspect ([data-model.md §4.1](./data-model.md)) |
| Creator | **ক্রিয়েটর / creator** (also শিক্ষক, লেখক, শিল্পী when specific) | "ইনফ্লুয়েন্সার" (English jargon), "ফান্ডরেইজার" | Plain, respectful, matches how people self-describe |
| Supporter | **সমর্থক / supporter**; anonymous display **একজন শুভানুধ্যায়ী / A well-wisher** | "ডোনার", "কাস্টমার", "ইউজার" | They are guests, not customers |
| Money | **টাকা** | ফান্ড, ওয়ালেট, ব্যালেন্স, পেমেন্ট (in our UI) | No wallet/balance concept exists — don't invent one in language |
| Content | **কাজ / লেখা / ভিডিও / ক্লাস** | "কনটেন্ট" (where avoidable) | Natural Bangla; name the actual thing |
| Page | **পেজ** | "প্রোফাইল", "স্টোর" | It is a page, not a shop |
| Creator's target | **লক্ষ্য / goal** (self-reported; optional) | "ক্যাম্পেইন", "ফান্ডরেইজার", "চ্যারিটি" | Excludes fundraising semantics |

**Pronouns and gender**
- Supporter-facing copy: **আপনি** always.
- Creator-facing copy: **আপনি** (Chanasta is respectful to creators too; the creator may use informal tone in their own tagline, but product chrome stays polite).
- Avoid gendered pronouns for creators in product copy. Use **ক্রিয়েটর** / **ক্রিয়েটরের** or the display name. Do not assume "তিনি/তাঁর" gender.
- Anonymous supporter: **একজন শুভানুধ্যায়ী / A well-wisher** — gender-neutral, warm.

## 6. The unit ladder (amount presets)

The presets make the decision small. Default to **৳20** selected. Creators may edit presets; the standard ladder is:

| # | Bangla | English | Amount (BDT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **এক কাপ চা** | A cup of cha | ৳20 |
| 2 | **এক প্লেট নাস্তা** | A plate of snacks | ৳50 |
| 3 | **খাবার** | A meal | ৳100 |
| 4 | **ইচ্ছেমতো** | Any amount | custom (min ৳1, max ৳100,000 — product guard) |

Rules
- Prices are **suggested metaphors, not menu prices**. Copy: *"দাম নয়, শুধু হিসাবের সুবিধা — যতটা ইচ্ছা পাঠাতে পারেন। / Not a price, just an easy way to choose — send any amount you like."*
- Show `৳20` style with Latin digits, no decimals.
- Custom amount field label: **ইচ্ছেমতো / Any amount**; helper: **৳1 থেকে শুরু / Starts at ৳1**.
- Presets are per-creator editable (max 6, per [data-model.md](./data-model.md)); the ladder above is the default set for new creators.
- MVP renders **no goal/progress UI** (Lead decision; the column is reserved in the data model). If it ships later, it must never read as "raised" or "collected" — it says **স্ব-ঘোষিত সমর্থন / self-reported support**.

## 9. Writing checklist (apply before any string ships)

- [ ] Bangla version sounds like a person from Dhaka, not a translation: read it aloud.
- [ ] English version means the same thing; it is not a word-for-word gloss.
- [ ] No banned words: donate/donation/দান, tip/টিপ, guaranteed/নিশ্চিত, verified payment/যাচাই করা পেমেন্ট, fund/fundraiser/ক্যাম্পেইন, wallet/ব্যালেন্স, escrow, refund/রিফান্ড, payout.
- [ ] Address form is **আপনি**; no gendered pronoun for the creator.
- [ ] The line "Chanasta doesn't touch the money" is present once on the page (footer strip at minimum).
- [ ] Amounts use `৳` + Latin digits (`৳20`), no decimals.
- [ ] No provider logos, no "official/partner" wording, provider-specific claims say "check current provider terms".
- [ ] Every empty/error state has an action (retry, go home, add method) — no dead ends.
- [ ] Copy fits on a 360px-wide screen without cramping (Bangla is wider; test both languages).
- [ ] No real names, numbers, TxIDs or QR payloads in any string, screenshot or test.
